## script 1

### Problem

Sometimes we need to solve math problems quickly, like in class or when shopping. Doing it by hand takes time and it's easy to make mistakes. This is a common problem for many people every day.

### Target User

This invention is for students who need to solve math during class. It's also useful for workers, shop owners, and anyone who needs quick, accurate calculations.

### Appearance

The calculator is small and rectangular, so it's easy to carry. It usually comes in simple colors like black or white. It has a small screen that shows numbers, and buttons that **give** users quick access to functions like plus, minus, multiply, and divide. Overall, it **offers** a simple and clean design that's easy to use.
